# Candidate Screening FAQ

### Why are you asking these questions?
They are used for preliminary qualification for the Junior Python/AI Engineer role.

### Does an internship count as experience?
Yes. Internships and academic projects are accepted for the experience question.

### Is machine learning mandatory?
No. Python is required. Machine learning/deep learning experience is preferred.

### Can I request a recruiter?
Yes. The candidate may request human assistance at any time.

### Does passing the screening guarantee an interview?
No. The screening is preliminary. Final interview decisions are made by a human recruiter.

### What salary will I receive?
The current screening knowledge base does not contain salary information. The assistant should not invent it.

### Can I work part-time?
The role is full-time, so part-time availability does not satisfy the stated requirement.
